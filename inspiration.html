Design Inspiration

What I'm learning from.

Good design taste is built, not born. This is a running shortlist of the books, people, and products that shape how I think about making things people actually want to use.

01 — On the shelf

Steal Like an Artist

Austin Kleon

Creativity as remix. Good work comes from collecting influences and making them your own, which is exactly what a page like this is for.

Against the Odds

James Dyson

Dyson's own story. 5,127 prototypes, and the case for being stubborn about getting a single thing right.

History of Great Inventions

Ed. Robert Uhlig

How the objects we take for granted actually came to be. Useful context for why some designs stick and others vanish.

The Design of Everyday Things

Don Norman

The foundation. Affordances, signifiers, and why a badly designed door needs a "push" sign to work.
