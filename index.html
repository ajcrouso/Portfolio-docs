01 — About

Bridging automotive rigor and consumer product thinking.

I'm a mechanical engineer focused on exterior components and mechanisms — the parts of a product that a person sees, touches, and judges in the first three seconds. My background is in automotive: exterior plastics, glass, and motion systems on the Honda FD3 platform, where tolerances are tight and the manufacturing bar is unforgiving.

My goal is to carry that discipline into consumer hardware — where the same skills in surfacing, tolerancing, and design-for-manufacturing decide whether a product feels premium or cheap. I care as much about how something feels and why as about what it does.
